# Operations and troubleshooting

## A range is supported by SAR but rejected or lost during extraction

Source intake now shares SAR's exact numeric grammar. Examples include `.05`,
`[1,10)`, `2..8`, `1e-3-2e-3` and censored bounds; the complete source cell or
text-row field is retained, not split at an interval comma, replaced by its
midpoint, or shifted into a neighbouring endpoint. Explicit source units remain
literal. The existing independent SAR adapter owns any permitted conversion;
unknown conditions/units and raw decimal-comma/uncertainty forms do not become
invented quantitative observations.

Malformed/reversed/empty intervals, unsupported suffixes and numeric budgets
refuse. Physical source ownership and actual-original replay remain mandatory;
unchanged graphs/identifiers are not evidence of a measurement's correctness.
Activity44/extractor56 prevent older extraction omissions from being reused as
current output. Keep originals, old task results and manual audit; fresh private
extraction still requires the normal authorized frontend path. This change
does not fix unresolved OCR headers or grant continuation from an unknown one.
Use the explicit measurement-literal, producer/original-replay, source-policy,
SAR interval/unit/statistics and tenth-strength checks, not a global test suite.

## Auxiliary OCR runs even though retained primary claims cannot agree

`not_attempted/conflicting_retained_primary_claims` means the existing retained
high-confidence part constraints are mutually incompatible: either both views
freeze different part counts, or they freeze differing literals at the same
position. Such a pair could not pass the existing final recovery validator.
The source wrapper now stops before runtime acquisition/original re-rendering;
direct complete-pair callers also stop before row inference. This avoids wasted
work; it does not correct the source, select a winning spelling or accept a title.

Both original readings and failed-attempt history remain saved. Unknown or
malformed observations are not labeled known conflicts, and malformed history
refuses rather than being replaced. Existing source/packet bounds still apply;
no evidence is trimmed to fit. Compatible weak sources keep normal paired
recovery and actual-original replay. Qualified primaries still do not load the
auxiliary runtime. No new model pass/install, epoch/SQL/API, activation or SAR
policy is needed. Focused controls include the source wrapper, direct header
caller, heading/binder entry, saved-source readers and installed consumers.

A stopped run can normally be resumed without `--force`; matching stage fingerprints are reused. Use `--force` only when the underlying PDF, rules or intended parameters changed materially.

Large scanned patents can spend several minutes in the first full-page OCR pass. The page cache is atomically checkpointed every ten completed pages, so after an interruption rerun without `--force` to resume it. A 146-page image-only WIPO sample exceeded a 10-minute verification window on this workstation; a three-page structure-table subset completed in 25 seconds, and its cached rerun completed in under one second. Treat first-pass OCR throughput as workload-dependent rather than a fixed service-level guarantee.

Activity extraction runs in an isolated subprocess with a bounded workload-aware timeout: 30 minutes minimum, 10 seconds per classified activity page, and 3 hours maximum. This prevents small jobs from hanging indefinitely while allowing large scanned patents to complete. A timeout remains a hard failure and must not be converted into partial acceptance.
